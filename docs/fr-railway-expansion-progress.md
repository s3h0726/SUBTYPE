# France railway expansion

The baseline is SUBTYPE `main` at `520e00ec0f00e114005b7a616dff16f049a7b148`. It contained no France country or route catalog. The evidence snapshot uses 2026-11-12.

## Playable batch

France has 160 selectable routes, 495 directions, and 1,636 Korean-localized station IDs. This is a verified playable subset, not nationwide completion. The earlier catalog includes nine domestic TGV INOUI corridors, two cross-border TGV INOUI corridors, Paris–Lausanne TGV Lyria, Bordeaux–Marseille daytime Intercités, Paris–Toulouse Intercités de nuit, five RER lines, and the regular full-length services of all Paris, Lyon, Rennes, Toulouse, Lille and Marseille Métro lines. Paris lines 7 and 13 include both branches. The two Métro 7 bis directions preserve their distinct stops on the terminal loop. A temporary works closure through 13 November was published for 7 bis. The schedule evidence covers its operating dates after that closure. [RATP service notice](https://www.ratp.fr/infos-trafic/metro/7B)

### National batch verified on 22 November 2026

The next nationwide batch adds 91 selectable lines and 195 directions, preserving all 300 previous direction IDs. Two additions extend the existing Paris–Dunkerque entry; the accepted batch therefore touches 92 catalog lines. Official SNCF resource 83582 supplies the ordered passenger calls. Its archived timetable was published on 9 November, collected on 12 November, and spans 11 November 2026–30 April 2027. Candidate operating dates were checked against 22 November; future restricted patterns retain their source dates. This is a pinned timetable snapshot, not a live disruption feed.

Additions include Paris–Lille, Paris–Tourcoing and Paris–Grenoble TGV, Paris–Nantes and Paris–Rennes OUIGO Train Classique, Nancy–Lyon Intercités, and regional branches across Hauts-de-France, Normandie, Grand Est, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine and Occitanie, including cross-border workings towards Tournai, Geneva and Portbou. Source corridor titles can exceed the playable endpoints: the Nantes–Bordeaux source contributes a verified Nantes–La Rochelle working; the Avignon–Portbou source contributes Nîmes–Portbou. These do not establish full coverage of the longer corridors. The reviewed regions in national-route-metadata.json describe the actual selected alignment.

OUIGO Train Classique uses the OSLO classification and a Korean conventional-train label, separately from high-speed OUIGO. Twenty timetable pattern records are reclassified by explicit source route IDs, including still-unreleased patterns. [SNCF OSLO](https://www.sncf-voyageurs.com/fr/decouvrez-notre-entreprise/nos-directions-et-filiales/oslo/), [OUIGO Train Classique](https://www.ouigo.com/infos-pratiques/ouigo-train-classique)

All 495 directions passed complete first-to-last Korean-answer gameplay on desktop and 390 px mobile, and every directed free-drive transition. All 160 route-specific Korean editing tables passed. Shared-name tests separately verified that editing Massy–Palaiseau propagates between the SNCF and IDFM identities into answers, destinations, signs, free drive and the editor. Saint-Lazare identities share edits while Haussmann retains its distinct station name. Country-switch tests cover Japan, Korea, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, Thailand, the United Kingdom and France. Geometry remains exact source nodes and ways. Different archived versions of the same OSM way receive deterministic snapshot keys rather than overwriting another route's geometry. Identical snapshots are shared. The catalog contains 41,678 physical ways and 41,700 geometry snapshots. Source names preserve French accents; Korean display fields use reviewed editorial names and remain editable.

The route-master search discovered 329 candidate masters and 1,103 member relations. Every member has an outcome: 202 already implemented, 195 verified additions, 662 blocked, and 44 unverifiable exports. Blocked candidates include unpaired directions, nonmatching calls, unsupported operating organizations, alternative geometry identities and expired patterns. The unparented-relation search timed out; this master search is not an exhaustive national inventory. The track audit examines 1,164 cached candidates against nine schedule resources. The wider 72-feed inventory contains 13,286 source-scoped stopping-pattern records, of which 501 have released geometry and full gameplay evidence; 12,785 remain. Overlapping feeds prevent interpreting that fraction as the percentage of distinct French operational lines. Corse, many tramways, independent railways, international patterns and other missing networks remain unfinished. All 13 regions and overseas candidates remain in the coverage inventory.

Operator totals count catalog classifications, including joint network and commercial-brand profiles; they are not a census of French legal operating organizations. ## Coverage and evidence

The per-feed denominator counts distinct ordered station calls and access restrictions within each source route. It includes overlapping regional and national feeds. The data does not yet support a nationwide percentage for distinct operational railway lines.

- [Coverage](../reports/fr/coverage.json) records source-scoped counts by service family and region, playable directions, tested directions, unimplemented source pattern IDs for partly implemented lines, and infrastructure totals.
- [Inventory](../reports/fr/inventory.json) covers the 13 metropolitan regions and identifies overseas feed candidates separately. The [source manifest](../data/fr/source/manifest.json) records publisher, URL, license, date window, archive hash and acquisition outcome where available.
- [Schedules](../data/fr/source/schedules/) contain per-feed line and direction outcomes. A raw `BLOCKED` pattern requires track and localization evidence. The coverage report overlays verified additions by their exact source pattern IDs.
- [Track audit](../reports/fr/track-evidence.json) contains examined OSM relations, matched timetable patterns and block reasons. In the selected Bordeaux–Marseille relations, Lacourtensourt remains part of the connected track. It is omitted from gameplay stops because the matching official Intercités runs pass it without passenger calls. The matched Marseille–Toulouse short workings still lack independently verified reverse alignments in the examined data.
- The RFN line and track inventories record physical line codes, operational infrastructure flags and track types. An infrastructure flag alone does not establish passenger operation. [SNCF Réseau line geometry](https://data.sncf.com/explore/dataset/formes-des-lignes-du-rfn/), [SNCF Réseau track geometry](https://ressources.data.sncf.com/explore/dataset/fichier-de-formes-des-voies-du-reseau-ferre-national/)

## Resume

Use Python 3.12 and Node.js 22 or newer. A cache can be outside the checkout. Keep the snapshot files if reproducing the original run, since publishers replace their downloads over time.

```sh
python -B tools/fr_fetch.py --cache ../fr-cache --include-unknown-mode
python -B tools/fr_inventory.py --cache ../fr-cache --as-of 2026-11-12 --baseline 520e00ec0f00e114005b7a616dff16f049a7b148
node tools/audit-fr-track-evidence.cjs ../fr-cache 83582 80921 81943 83281 81678 81995 39589 81474 79818
python -B tools/fr_national_track_discovery.py --masters data/fr/source/osm-national-masters.json --cache ../fr-cache --output data/fr/source/national-track-discovery.json
node tools/prepare-fr-national.cjs ../fr-cache 2026-11-22
node tools/build-fr.cjs
node tools/test-france-data.cjs
node tools/build-app-bundle.js
node tools/test-france-browser.cjs
node tools/report-fr-coverage.cjs
node tools/finalize-fr-national.cjs
node tools/report-fr-coverage.cjs
```

`python -B tools/fr_transilien_discovery.py --cache ../fr-cache` enumerates the official feed’s Transilien lines, checks their configured OSM master identities, downloads every child train relation, and compares identical saved sources against recorded implementation outcomes. `--offline` reuses the saved cache; `--refresh` refreshes OSM exports. The output is a candidate inventory in the cache and does not approve routes for gameplay. Regenerate the official schedule snapshot with the national fetch/inventory tools before a new timetable period.

The track audit accepts OpenStreetMap full relation exports named `fr-osm-<relation-id>.json` in the cache. For each accepted direction, retain the source relation in `data/fr/source/osm/` and add the candidate to `data/fr/source/release-lines.json`. Additional stopping patterns on the same track can be bound through `data/fr/source/track-call-variants.json`. Each selection must match a current official sequence and retain the mapped track through any skipped station. Review aliases and Korean names before building. The browser test uses Playwright. Set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` and `CHROME_PATH` if they are installed outside the project. Set `FRANCE_PUBLIC_URL` to exercise representative directions on the published site.

`node tools/report-fr-production.cjs` combines the identified production reports, checks that each recorded direction passed all three modes, rejects unknown direction IDs, and requires every released Transilien direction to have a recorded production run. It reports the unique tested IDs across the recorded releases separately from a complete test of the latest public catalog.
