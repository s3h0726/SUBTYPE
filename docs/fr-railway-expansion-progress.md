# France railway expansion

The baseline is SUBTYPE `main` at `520e00ec0f00e114005b7a616dff16f049a7b148`. It contained no France country or route catalog. The evidence snapshot uses 2026-11-12.

## Playable batch

France has 60 selectable routes, 201 directions, and 951 Korean-localized station IDs. The catalog includes nine domestic TGV INOUI corridors, two cross-border TGV INOUI corridors, Paris–Lausanne TGV Lyria, Bordeaux–Marseille daytime Intercités, Paris–Toulouse Intercités de nuit, five RER lines, and the regular full-length services of all Paris, Lyon, Rennes, Toulouse, Lille and Marseille Métro lines. Paris lines 7 and 13 include both branches. The two Métro 7 bis directions preserve their distinct stops on the terminal loop. A temporary works closure through 13 November was published for 7 bis. The schedule evidence covers its operating dates after that closure. [RATP service notice](https://www.ratp.fr/infos-trafic/metro/7B)

- RER A covers all five termini with twelve directions across six scheduled branch pairings. B covers the airport through services to Robinson and Saint-Rémy, Mitry through services to Robinson and Saint-Rémy, and the Gare du Nord workings to Mitry and the airport. The verified Saint-Rémy-to-airport all-stops working runs on 21–22 November in this timetable snapshot. D covers five bidirectional workings serving Creil, Orry-la-Ville, Goussainville, Juvisy, Malesherbes, Corbeil-Essonnes and Melun. E covers both eastern branches from Nanterre to Tournan and Chelles. [RATP RER A](https://www.ratp.fr/vos-lignes/rer/a), [SNCF RER B](https://www.transilien.com/fr/page-lignes/ligne-b), [SNCF RER D](https://www.transilien.com/fr/page-lignes/ligne-d), [SNCF RER E](https://www.transilien.com/fr/page-lignes/ligne-e)
- RER C covers seven bidirectional workings. Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines connects to Dourdan, Dourdan la Forêt and Saint-Martin d’Étampes. The other workings connect Versailles Château Rive Gauche to Juvisy, Chaville–Vélizy to Bibliothèque François Mitterrand and Invalides to Brétigny or Dourdan. Each geometry joins three original OSM relations at identical stop node IDs and coordinates. Short workings use the mapped platform nodes as bounds. The local GTFS sequence determines the calls along the joined track. Invalides stop names come from the OSM stop area shared by both stop positions. [SNCF RER C](https://www.transilien.com/fr/page-lignes/ligne-c), [Invalides stop area](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3323339)
- The next RER C batch adds eighteen directions, covering Pontoise and Montigny–Beauchamp to Massy–Palaiseau, Thiais–Orly, Invalides and Avenue Henri Martin, plus Austerlitz–Massy and Austerlitz–Thiais workings. Date-restricted patterns have their own Korean service labels. The Choisy junction uses the identical OSM track node on each side of the connection. Thiais–Orly's outbound stop was already mapped on the selected way and is bound explicitly in the correction manifest. A six-stop-skipping pattern operated only on 12 November and is excluded from this batch. [SNCF RER C network](https://www.transilien.com/fr/page-lignes/ligne-c), [November Pontoise service notices](https://monrercetlignev.transilien.com/events/categorie/paris-pontoise/)

## Coverage and evidence

The per-feed denominator counts distinct ordered station calls and access restrictions within each source route. It includes overlapping regional and national feeds. The data does not yet support a nationwide percentage for distinct operational railway lines.

- [Coverage](../reports/fr/coverage.json) records source-scoped counts by service family and region, playable directions, tested directions, unimplemented source pattern IDs for partly implemented lines, and infrastructure totals.
- [Inventory](../reports/fr/inventory.json) covers the 13 metropolitan regions and identifies overseas feed candidates separately. The [source manifest](../data/fr/source/manifest.json) records publisher, URL, license, date window, archive hash and acquisition outcome where available.
- [Schedules](../data/fr/source/schedules/) contain per-feed line and direction outcomes. A raw `BLOCKED` pattern requires track and localization evidence. The coverage report overlays verified additions by their exact source pattern IDs.
- [Track audit](../reports/fr/track-evidence.json) contains examined OSM relations, matched timetable patterns and block reasons. In the selected Bordeaux–Marseille relations, Lacourtensourt remains part of the connected track. It is omitted from gameplay stops because the matching official Intercités runs pass it without passenger calls. The matched Marseille–Toulouse short workings still lack independently verified reverse alignments in the examined data.
- The RFN line and track inventories record physical line codes, operational infrastructure flags and track types. An infrastructure flag alone does not establish passenger operation. [SNCF Réseau line geometry](https://data.sncf.com/explore/dataset/formes-des-lignes-du-rfn/), [SNCF Réseau track geometry](https://ressources.data.sncf.com/explore/dataset/fichier-de-formes-des-voies-du-reseau-ferre-national/)

## Resume

Use Python 3.12 and Node.js 22 or newer. A cache can be outside the checkout. Keep the snapshot files if reproducing the original run, since publishers replace their downloads over time.

```sh
python -B tools/fr_fetch.py --cache ../fr-cache --include-unknown-mode
python -B tools/fr_inventory.py --cache ../fr-cache --as-of 2026-11-12 --baseline 520e00ec0f00e114005b7a616dff16f049a7b148
node tools/audit-fr-track-evidence.cjs ../fr-cache 83582 80921 81943 83281 81678 81995 39589 81474 79818
node tools/build-fr.cjs
node tools/test-france-data.cjs
node tools/build-app-bundle.js
node tools/test-france-browser.cjs
node tools/report-fr-coverage.cjs
```

The track audit accepts OpenStreetMap full relation exports named `fr-osm-<relation-id>.json` in the cache. For each accepted direction, retain the source relation in `data/fr/source/osm/` and add the candidate to `data/fr/source/release-lines.json`. Additional stopping patterns on the same track can be bound through `data/fr/source/track-call-variants.json`. Each selection must match a current official sequence and retain the mapped track through any skipped station. Review aliases and Korean names before building. The browser test uses Playwright. Set `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` and `CHROME_PATH` if they are installed outside the project. Set `FRANCE_PUBLIC_URL` to exercise representative directions on the published site.

The next eligible work is the remaining high-speed and OUIGO patterns, then Intercités daytime and overnight directions and all TER regions. Follow with the missing Île-de-France modes, regional metros and tramways, Corsica, independent railways and international services. Compare overlapping source line identities, especially for regional aggregates and SNCF. Additional work remains on NeTEx-only services, operator feeds with missing or unavailable schedules, local systems outside the national catalog, and the overseas inventory. Compare timetable service, infrastructure, and special railway public access independently before releasing any heritage or mountain railway.

The RER audit covers route masters `2333448` (A), `7420701` (B), `122886` (C), `1673421` (D) and `7271953` (E). B still needs independently verified track approaches for the Massy terminal workings and additional stop patterns on the airport branch. C still has additional through workings and restricted stopping patterns to verify. For the verified Choisy junction, relations `3019053` and `3007168` share track node `600739778` outbound, and `3007169` and `3019054` share node `600739774` inbound. The E Chelles relations `65456` and `9488694` omit four OSM ways on the Noisy-le-Sec–Strasbourg railway. Each recorded insertion connects the exact end nodes on both sides of an omission. The original relations and supplemental ways are saved separately. IDFM currently schedules the opening to Mantes-la-Jolie for 2027-02-28. [IDFM RER E extension announcement](https://presse.iledefrance-mobilites.fr/a-moins-de-120-jours-du-prolongement-du-rer-e-vers-mantes-la-jolie-le-nouvel-atelier-de-maintenance-entre-en-service/?lang=fr)
