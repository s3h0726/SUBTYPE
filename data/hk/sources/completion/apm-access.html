Special Needs Access

Accessibility for All

ACI’s Accessibility Enhancement Accreditation Program
The HKIA was accredited by ACI’s Accessibility Enhancement Accreditation Program with the highest possible Level 3 ranking since 2023. It was recognised the airport’s continued efforts to provide accessibility services and facilities for passengers with disabilities and reduced mobility. We offer a Courtesy Channel for expedited entry to security screening and immigration, phone charging facilities at accessible height, braille maps and tactile guide paths, as well as audible warning signals and announcements in all people conveyance facilities, such as lifts, escalators and walkways.

Hidden Disabilities Sunflower Program
Starting from 1 September 2026, HKIA officially joined the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower Program, which provides support to passengers with non-visible disabilities or conditions. Wearing the Sunflower symbol helps our airport staff to identify and offer appropriate assistance throughout the airport journey. This initiative further reinforces HKIA’s commitment to creating an inclusive and welcoming environment for all passengers.

  • Wheelchair Service

    Passengers who wish to use wheelchair service at the Hong Kong International Airport may pre-book the complimentary service by contacting their airlines or travel agents.

    With pre-booking, the airlines will arrange for passengers needing a wheelchair to be picked up at an agreed location, such as the terminal kerbside, the Airport Express Line platform or the check-in counter, depending on the request made in advance.

    Alternatively, passengers can also procure and pre-book the wheelchair service at a charge from the airport-licensed service provider ALLWAYS 24-hour hotline at +852 2275 0000.

    If passengers wish to travel with their own battery-powered wheelchairs, please refer to the section on “Battery-powered mobility aids” (e.g. wheelchairs) in Packing Tips for Air Passengers issued by the Civil Aviation Department (CAD). Please note that carriage on aircraft of battery-powered wheelchairs is subject to the consent of the relevant airlines. Individual airlines may set requirements which may be different from, or in additional to, the CAD’s recommended guidelines in Packing Tips for Air Passengers. Passengers are advised to contact the airline(s) concerned for further assistance and confirmation of applicable requirements before departure. Contact details for airlines can be found here.

  • Arriving the Airport
    By bus/ taxi/ coach

    The following facilities are available at the departures kerbs at Terminals 1 (T1) and Terminal 2 (T2)​.

    • Priority drop-off areas with ramps
    • Special needs serving points with tactile guide paths and Braille maps (Zones 2 and 3 in T1, Zone 9 in T2​​)
    • Hands-free help phones are provided at Zones 2 and 3 of T1, Zones 6-9 of T2​
    • Ramps with handrails provide accessibility from the departures kerbs to the Check-in Hall (Level 7 in T1 and T2)
     
    By Airport Express

    The following facilities are available to guide you to the Check-in Hall upon your arrival at HKIA by Airport Express (AEL).

    • Tactile guide paths that lead you from the AEL platform to the nearest Customer Service Counter
    • Ramps with handrails provide accessibility from the departure kerbs to the Check-in Hall (Level 7 in T1 and T2)
  • Security Screening
    Passengers with special needs can use the Courtesy Channel located at T1 and T2 to enter the security screening and immigration area.
  • Immigration clearance

    The following facilities are available at the immigration area for passengers with special needs.

    • Wide self-service e-channels for Hong Kong residents and “qualified” visitors
    • Counters for passengers with special needs
    • Seats for passengers waiting for assistance with immigration clearance

  • Sunflower Program

    Passengers with hidden disabilities, health conditions or additional needs may choose to wear a Sunflower symbol as a discreet indication that they may require additional support, understanding, patience or assistance during their airport journey. Participation is voluntary, and passengers are not required to disclose their condition or provide medical documentation. In HKIA, Sunflower lanyards are available free of charge at 5 designated Customer Services Centres.

    HKIA Hidden Disabilities Sunflower Lanyard Distribution Points

    Terminal 1

    Terminal 2
    • Front of Check-in Aisle E, Departures Level (L7)
    • East Hall, After Departures, Departures Level (L6)
    • West Hall, After Departures, Departures Level (L6)
    • Arrivals Hall, Arrivals Level (L5)
    • Front of Check-in Aisle T, Departures Level (L7)

    In addition, the Sensory Corner near Gate 10 is available to all passengers with sensory needs, whether or not they are wearing a Sunflower symbol.
