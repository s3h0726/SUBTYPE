The Railways Branch under EMSD is the regulatory authority on railway safety in Hong Kong. The Railways Branch regulates and monitors the safe operation of the following railway system:

  • Railway systems operated by the MTR Corporation Limited
  • Automated People Mover at the Hong Kong International Airport
  • Tramway system operated by Hong Kong Tramways
  • Peak tramway system operated by Peak Tramways Company Limited

The main functions of the Railways Branch include:

  • ensuring the adoption of appropriate safety practices by the railway operators;
  • investigating into safety-related railway incidents and following up the operators' improvement measures;
  • assessing and approving new railway projects and modifications to the existing major railway facilities;
  • providing professional support on railway safety to government bureaux and other government departments;
  • handling complaints and enquiries on matters related to railway safety; and
  • promoting public railway safety.
