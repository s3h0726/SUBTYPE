Automated People Mover

The Automated People Mover takes passengers between Terminal 2, Terminal 1, T1 Midfield Concourse and SkyPier Terminal. Travelling at approximately 60 kilometres per hour, this driverless electric train system carries about 7,200 passengers per hour and departs every few minutes.

Running Between Terminal 2, Terminal 1 and T1 Midfield Concourse : 05:30 – 00:30

Running Between Terminal 1 and SkyPier Terminal : 06:15 – 23:45

Tips for passengers to Boarding Gate 201-230 (With APM Operation)

Tips for passengers to Boarding Gate 201-230 (during non-APM Operation)
